Clinical Research-Gap Formula
Gap(C) = α (1 − centrality) + β · uncertainty + γ · (1 − evidence)
α = 0.20 low centrality weight β = 0.30 uncertainty weight γ = 0.20 weak evidence weight degree−1 × 0.30 low connectivity weight
Gaps are claims where G(C) is highest — not LLM opinion. Graph decides; LLM labels.
